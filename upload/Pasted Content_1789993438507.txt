# 1. PRODUCT VISION

Build a centralized **Social Media Operations & Organization Communications Platform** for professional teams managing multiple authorized social-media accounts, content workflows, staff tasks, analytics, community management and organization email infrastructure.

The fundamental workflow is:

**CONNECT → ORGANIZE → CREATE → REVIEW → APPROVE → PUBLISH → MONITOR → ENGAGE → ANALYZE → REPORT**

The platform should eliminate unnecessary manual switching between multiple social-media dashboards.

A team member should be able to log into one secure application and access everything they are authorized to manage.
